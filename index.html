Methodology & NASA POWER Data Lineage

Designed specifically to withstand peer review and climate forensics scrutiny. Every calculation uses open NASA POWER reanalysis and satellite-derived observation data with zero synthetic values.

01 :: FETCH

NASA POWER REST

Automated monthly point ingestion from NASA Langley Research Center POWER API.

02 :: CLEAN

Mask Fill & QA

Missing fill values (-999.0) excluded. Time-series completeness verified (< 20% missing threshold).

03 :: DECOMPOSE

Monthly climatology removal

Calendar 12-month climatology stripped to eliminate seasonal cycle oscillations and isolate secular anomalies.

04 :: TEST

Yue-Pilon TFPW MK

Lag-1 serial autocorrelation pre-whitened to eliminate false alarms and validate true monotonic drift.

05 :: ESTIMATE

Theil-Sen Slope

Median of all pairwise slopes computes decadal trajectory magnitude resistant to extreme episodic outliers.

SCIENTIFIC CAVEAT & CITATION

A statistically significant monotonic trend is NOT standalone proof of human causation. Trends document empirical physical trajectory deviations over the 2001–2024 satellite era. All inferences require grounding alongside localized socio-ecological and land-use ground truth.
